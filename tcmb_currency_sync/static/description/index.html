TCMB Currency Sync

Automatic USD & EUR exchange rates from the Turkish Central Bank

Overview

Turkish businesses using Odoo need accurate, up-to-date exchange rates for sales orders, invoices, and financial reports. This module eliminates manual rate entry by pulling official ForexBuying rates directly from the Turkish Central Bank (TCMB) XML feed and syncing them into Odoo automatically every day.

Features

  • Fetches official USD and EUR rates from tcmb.gov.tr
  • Syncs automatically every day via Odoo's built-in scheduler
  • Updates existing rates — no duplicate entries
  • Zero configuration needed after installation
  • Compatible with Odoo 18 Community & Enterprise (On-Premise)

How to Verify

After installation, go to Accounting → Configuration → Currencies, open USD or EUR, and check the Rates tab to confirm automatic updates.
